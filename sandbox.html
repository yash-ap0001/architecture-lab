Sandbox help

  • Add: click a component, or drag it onto the board.
  • Connect: drag from the dot on a box's right edge to another box, or use ↔ Connect and click two boxes.
  • Move: drag a box. Pan: drag empty space, or hold Space. Zoom: mouse wheel.
  • Edit: click a box, then the Selected tab. Delete: Delete key. Undo: Ctrl+Z.
  • Run: ▶ Start. Change traffic, speed and failures while it runs.
  • The model is a teaching model; its numbers are not benchmarks. Edit any number in Selected, and mark numbers you measured in the design brief.
