Sandbox help

  • Add: click a component, or drag it onto the board.
  • Connect: drag from the dot on a box's right edge to another box, or use ↔ Connect and click two boxes.
  • Move: drag a box. Pan: drag empty space, or hold Space. Zoom: mouse wheel. Minimap: click it to jump.
  • Select several: Shift-click boxes or Shift-drag on empty space; drag one to move them all. Ctrl+A selects everything, Ctrl+D duplicates, L locks or unlocks, Esc clears.
  • Edit: click a box; its settings open in the Inspect panel. Delete: Delete key. Undo: Ctrl+Z.
  • Run: ▶ Start. Change traffic, speed and failures while it runs.
  • The model is a teaching model; its numbers are not benchmarks. Edit any number in Inspect, and mark numbers you measured in the design brief.
